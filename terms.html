Not financial advice

Pay Off Your House Fast is a calculator. It projects figures from numbers you enter, using standard amortization maths and assumptions you can change. It is not financial, mortgage, tax or legal advice, it is not a loan offer, and it is not a pre-approval. Before acting on anything you see in the app, confirm the figures with your lender or a qualified adviser.

The affordability calculator uses ratios that lenders commonly start from. A real lending decision also depends on your credit history, employment, reserves and the property itself.

Results are only as good as the numbers entered and the assumptions made. Actual results vary with the exact date payments are applied, rate changes, escrow adjustments and servicer policy. We make no warranty that any projection will be achieved.
