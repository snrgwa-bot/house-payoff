Enter your balance, rate and payment once. Pay Off Your House Fast shows your payoff date, how much interest is still ahead, and exactly how much sooner a few extra dollars a month gets you there. Everything stays on your iPhone.

What it does

Your payoff date, in plain sight

One screen: the month your mortgage ends, the share you have already paid off, the interest still to come, and the total left to pay.

Six ways to finish sooner

Extra each month, round up the payment, one extra payment a year, biweekly, weekly, or a single lump sum. Each option shows its own payoff date and saving before you pick it.

Honest biweekly maths

Biweekly and weekly plans are modelled on their real 26 and 52 payment periods, not a monthly approximation, so the numbers hold up.

The true cost of owning

Property tax, insurance, HOA dues and a maintenance reserve alongside the loan — including what carries on after the mortgage is gone.

A schedule you can print

The full amortization schedule, grouped by year, with a PDF export.

What you can afford

Work backwards from income, deposit and existing debts — credit cards, car and student loans — to a realistic home price, and see which lender rule caps you.

Private by design

No account. No bank connection. No tracking. Your loan details are written to your iPhone and never leave it. There is no server to hold them, and we have no way to read them.

Pricing

The payoff date, the extra-payment simulator, the amortization schedule and the affordability calculator are free. Pro unlocks the other payoff strategies, the true cost of ownership and the printable PDF export.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and are managed in your Apple ID settings.
