Home

Your payoff date, in plain sight.

The month your mortgage ends, the share already paid off, the interest still to come and the total left to pay — the first thing you see.

  • Payoff ring and equity at a glance
  • Log extra payments as you make them
  • Milestones at 25, 50, 75% and mortgage-free
Plan

Six ways to finish sooner.

Extra each month on a slider, round up the payment, one extra payment a year, biweekly, weekly, or a single lump sum. Every option shows its own payoff date and saving before you pick it.

  • Biweekly and weekly modelled on real 26 and 52 periods
  • Time saved measured between actual payoff dates
Schedule

A schedule you can print.

The full amortization schedule grouped by year, with a plan-versus-base toggle so you can see every payment move. Export it as a PDF for your records or your lender.

Pro

The true cost of owning.

Property tax, insurance, HOA dues and a maintenance reserve alongside the loan — including what carries on every month after the mortgage is gone. Log repairs so surprises are budgeted.

Buying next?

Know what you can afford.

Work backwards from income, deposit and existing debts — credit cards, car and student loans — to a realistic price. See which lender rule caps you, and what clearing a debt would buy.
